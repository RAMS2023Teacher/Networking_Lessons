# Scope and sequence

Written by `node tools/scope.mjs` from the lesson files. Do not edit by hand.

Standards are from the Massachusetts Digital Literacy and Computer Science framework, grades 6 to 8. All three grades share one set of standards, so the courses spiral: grade 6 builds and names the parts, grade 7 follows data across the internet, grade 8 protects and designs.

## Grade 7: How the Internet Works

Addresses, names, packets, routing, protocols and the cloud.

| # | Lesson | Question of the day | Students can say | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A Network of Networks | What is the internet actually made of? | I can explain that the internet is many networks joined by routers, with more than one path between places. | 6-8.CS.c.1, 6-8.CS.c.2 |
| 2 | Every Device Needs an Address | How does data know where to go? | I can explain what an IP address is and use one to deliver data to the right device. | 6-8.CS.c.3, 6-8.CT.a.1 |
| 3 | Names for Numbers: DNS | How does a name you type become an address a router can use? | I can describe how DNS turns a name into an IP address. | 6-8.CS.c.3 |
| 4 | Packets | Why does the internet chop everything into pieces? | I can explain how a message is split into packets and put back together. | 6-8.CS.c.3 |
| 5 | Routing | How does a packet find its way across the world? | I can route a packet across a network and compare different paths. | 6-8.CS.c.2, 6-8.CT.b.4, 6-8.CT.b.2 |
| 6 | Lost and Found | What happens when a packet never arrives? | I can explain how networks notice lost packets and recover from them. | 6-8.CS.c.3, 6-8.CT.b.5 |
| 7 | Protocols: Rules of the Road | How do devices from different companies understand each other? | I can explain what a protocol is and name what HTTP, DNS, TCP and IP each do. | 6-8.CS.c.3 |
| 8 | Speed: Bandwidth and Latency | What does "fast internet" really mean? | I can tell bandwidth and latency apart and predict how each affects what I do online. | 6-8.CS.c.1, 6-8.CT.b.2 |
| 9 | The Cloud | Where is "the cloud," really? | I can explain what the cloud is and what devices can do because of network services. | 6-8.CS.d.1, 6-8.CS.c.3 |
| 10 | Trace a Web Request | What happens in the half second after you press Enter? | I can trace a web request from my device to a server and back, naming each step. | 6-8.CS.c.3, 6-8.CS.c.2, 6-8.CT.b.3 |
